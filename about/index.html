About the Project

The Forgotten Places Project was started in July 2026, by a person known online as Aellic. It is meant to be an organized and easily navigated database of abandoned locations around the world. Every location has a unique history, and the Forgotten Places Project is dedicated to unearthing history that would otherwise be lost to time.

About Aellic

Aellic is, at the time of founding the Forgotten Places Project, 21 years old. They have several interests and hobbies as a result of their neurodivergence and their childhood with neurodivergent parents. Their main hobby is computer science, having graduated high school at the age of 17 with 8 computer science credits, including 2 college-level credits. In late April 2026, Aellic was laying in bed, watching Youtube while fixing a phone for their roommate's friend, when a content creator known as "2swag" came up on their recommended feed, thus starting Aellic's discovery of urban exploration, also known as UrbEx. Aellic is currently a student in a trade school, learning to work in the construction field, and is unable to travel much as a result, but is pursuing training in the transportation field in the hopes of gaining employment with a train line, which will unlock much more opportunity for travel. Until then, however, Aellic wants to use their enthusiasm for technology and research to grow the Forgotten Places Project, to make it just a bit easier for people to find abandoned locations in their area, learn the history behind the locations, and maybe go out and have a bit of fun (in areas where the law allows it).
