What is the Forgotten Places Project?

Have you ever seen an abandoned building and wondered about it? What was it? Why was it built? And why was it abandoned? The Forgotten Places Project is dedicated to finding and documenting these places, so that you don't have to keep wondering. Time does great things, and although the Project is still small at the moment, it will only grow.
